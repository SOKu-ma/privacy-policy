DartLog Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how SO-KUMA Labs ("we", "us", or "the developer") handles information in DartLog ("the App").

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. Information Handled by the App

The App may handle the following information:

  • Practice records, including the selected target, number of rounds, dart landing coordinates, hit or miss results, intended target information, and round notes.
  • Game records for X01, Count-Up, Cricket, and Around the Clock, including scores, turns, dart landing coordinates when available, game settings, intended or system-recommended target information, and notes.
  • History, dates, identifiers, in-progress game data, and statistics or analysis calculated from the records above.
  • App settings such as the selected theme and, where shown during development, a language preference.
  • If the future Auto Capture concept screen is available, the user's optional multiple-choice interest, expected use location, and candidate price response. These responses are stored on the device only; the camera feature itself is not provided in the initial release.
  • Purchase and subscription status information needed to provide Premium features through Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat.
  • Information the user chooses to enter and submit through the inquiry form.

The App does not require an account and does not intentionally collect the user's name, email address, contacts, photos, videos, or precise location during ordinary use.

2. Local Records and the Developer's Server

DartLog is designed as a local-first app. Practice records, game records, landing coordinates, scores, notes, intended targets, history, and locally calculated analysis are stored in the App's local database on the user's device. The initial release has no developer-operated account system, cloud sync, or server feature for uploading these records. The developer does not receive the raw records through the App.

The operating system may include App data in a device backup, migration, or sharing action started by the user. Such processing is controlled by the relevant operating system or recipient and is outside the App's ordinary server processing.

3. Third-Party Services

Apple App Store and Google Play: These stores process payment and subscription transactions. Store account, order, receipt, subscription, and billing information may be handled by Apple or Google under their respective terms and privacy policies. The App and the developer do not receive or store the user's card number or other store payment credentials.

RevenueCat: The App uses RevenueCat to manage Premium entitlement status and restore purchases. RevenueCat may handle an SDK-generated app-specific identifier and information such as the store, product, purchase, subscription, entitlement, renewal, and expiration status needed for that purpose. This service is not used to receive the App's practice or game records.

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics: These services are not integrated into the initial release. Accordingly, the initial release does not send DartLog usage events or crash reports to Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. If this changes, this Policy will be updated before the relevant processing begins.

4. Inquiry Form and Device or App Information

When the user chooses Contact us, the App opens the common Google Form used for app support. The App may prefill visible and editable fields with the app name, operating system, OS version, app version, build number, store, and device model. The user may also voluntarily enter an inquiry type, details, email address, and other information in the form. The user decides whether to submit the form.

The App does not attach practice records, game records, landing coordinates, scores, notes, intended targets, passwords, payment credentials, or hidden identifiers to the inquiry form. Submitted information is sent to the form operator, currently Google, and is handled under the operator's terms and privacy policy.

5. Information and Permissions Not Used in the Initial Release

  • No advertising SDK or interest-based advertising is used.
  • No precise or background location is collected.
  • No contacts or address book data is collected.
  • No camera, photo library, or video recording/analysis is provided or used in the initial release.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking technology is used by the App.

The optional Auto Capture interest responses are limited to the choices shown on that screen and are saved locally. They are not a camera recording feature, a survey submission to an external service, or a transmission of dartboard images.

6. Retention and Deletion

Local records and local settings are retained on the device until the user deletes the App data or uninstalls the App. The current release does not provide a developer-side account or server copy to delete. Deleting the App or clearing its storage may remove local records, but records restored from an operating-system backup are subject to that backup's settings and lifecycle.

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat retain purchase and subscription information for the periods and purposes described in their policies and as needed for transaction, entitlement, security, fraud-prevention, or legal purposes. Inquiry submissions are retained by the form operator and, when received by the developer, only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, and address related quality or legal matters; information that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymized where reasonably practicable.

To ask about an inquiry submission or request handling under applicable law, use the Contact Form below. Requests cannot delete local records from the user's device; the user must delete those records on the device or through its operating-system storage controls.

7. Third-Party Disclosure and Processing Outside Japan

We do not sell the App's records and do not provide them to third parties for their own advertising. Information is disclosed only when the user submits it to an external service, when necessary to provide store-based subscriptions, or when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or prevent fraud. Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and Google Forms may process information outside Japan in accordance with their own operations and policies. We do not operate a separate server for DartLog records in the initial release.

9. Children and Minors

The App is not directed specifically to children. Minors should use the App with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian where required by applicable law. If a minor submits an inquiry, the guardian may contact us through the form below.
